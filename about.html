Our story

Pakistan's own coffee, started by a 20-year-old in Karachi

In October 2023, during Pakistan's nationwide boycott movement, Mahnoor Rajput decided to support brands with a purpose. On one grocery trip she noticed something surprising: there wasn't a single Pakistani instant coffee brand on the shelf. Store after store, it was the same.

So she decided that if no one else was making it, she would. That decision became Koffeely.

Koffeely brings international-grade coffee to Pakistan. Our beans come from Brazil and Ethiopia, two of the world's best coffee regions, and every jar is 100% coffee with no additives or fillers. Later we added Midori, a ceremonial-grade Japanese matcha, for the days you want calm energy instead of a jolt.

“I wanted Pakistan to have a coffee brand we could trust, one that competes globally but stays true to our values.”

Mahnoor Rajput, founder
