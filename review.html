I went through koffeely.co page by page and found 25 problems. Some are broken or left over from the Shopify template. Others quietly cost you Google traffic and sales. I also built a working redesign that fixes all of them, using your real products, prices and photos.

What I found

Everything here was checked on the live site on 1 October 2026. Each item says what's wrong, why it matters and how the redesign handles it.

Broken or unfinished visitors notice these

  1. Shopify demo text is still live

    Broken

    The Recipes and #CrazyKoffeeClub pages both open with “We make things that work better and last longer.” That line is placeholder copy from the theme.

    Fix: every page has real Koffeely copy.

  2. Recipes and #CrazyKoffeeClub are the same page, and neither has content

    Broken

    Both pages are identical: one line of text and a “Shop Koffeely” button. There are no recipes on the Recipes page.

    Fix: 8 real recipes with measurements, and a proper club page.

  3. The menu sends people to the wrong pages

    Broken

    “#CrazyKoffeeClub” opens the Shop page. “About Us” opens a blog list titled “News”. The footer's “Contact us” opens the legal contact-information page instead of the contact form.

    Fix: every link goes where it says.

  4. Refund policy still says “[INSERT RETURN ADDRESS]”

    Broken

    The policy is Shopify's template, written for clothing (“unworn, with tags”), with an EU section that doesn't apply. It offers 30-day returns, then says there are no refunds. Customers and payment providers read this page.

    Fix: a short, honest returns answer in the FAQ. The policy itself needs rewriting in Shopify.

  5. There is no shipping policy

    Broken

    /policies/shipping-policy returns “Page not found”. Delivery time, charges and cash on delivery aren't explained anywhere on the site.

    Fix: a delivery FAQ on the contact page, marked where details need your confirmation.

  6. Typo in the recipes address and title

    Broken

    The page lives at /pages/recepies and its browser title reads “Recepies – Koffeely”.

  7. The founder's surname doesn't match

    Broken

    The founder post calls her “Mahnoor Rajput” in the text but is signed “Mahnoor Sagheer”.

    Fix: one name everywhere (Rajput, as in the article; please confirm).

  8. A skincare graphic on the tumbler page

    Broken

    Two tumbler photos are files named Product-Features-Skincare-Face-Cream-Benefits…, left over from a template. Another is a 200 × 300 thumbnail that looks blurry when zoomed.

    Fix: only real product photos are used.

  9. Messy product data

    Broken

    “The Midori Ritual Kit” has a double space in its name. Its description calls it a “Starter Kit”. Matcha has a “compare at” price equal to its real price. One product is tagged “Uncategorized”, and no product has a type set.

Search and sharing costs you Google traffic

  1. The homepage title and description are just “Koffeely”

    SEO

    Google, WhatsApp and Instagram link previews show nothing about what you sell. Nobody searching “instant coffee Pakistan” is told this is the page for them.

    Fix: each page has its own title and description.

  2. The share image is a thin 500 × 171 logo

    SEO

    When someone sends your link on WhatsApp, the preview is a small wordmark on white.

    Fix: a 1200 × 630 photo of your jars.

  3. Three main headings (H1) on the homepage

    SEO

    The strongest one is “ABOUT KOFFEELY CO.”, which tells Google nothing about the product.

    Fix: one clear H1 per page.

  4. No structured data

    SEO

    Google can't show your prices, stock or star ratings in search results.

    Fix: Organization, Product and FAQ data built in.

  5. Every photo of a product has the same alt text

    SEO

    No photo has its own description, so the theme reuses the product name. On the chasen page, “Midori Chasen – Bamboo Matcha Whisk” appears 15 times. Screen readers and Google Images can't tell the photos apart.

    Fix: each photo describes what it shows.

  6. Camera-roll file names and a WhatsApp favicon

    SEO

    Image files are named like IMG_6032.jpg and image00001.jpg. The browser-tab icon is WhatsApp_Image_2025-10-07…jpg.

    Fix: descriptive file names like midori-chasen-whisk.webp and a proper icon.

  7. An emoji in the blog address

    SEO

    The founder post lives at /blogs/news/%E2%98%95meet-mahnoor…. It looks broken when shared.

Lost sales the biggest money impact

  1. No WhatsApp ordering

    Sales

    In Pakistan, many customers would rather message than fill in a checkout. There's no WhatsApp button anywhere.

    Fix: a floating WhatsApp button, plus “Order on WhatsApp” on every product and in the cart, with the order pre-filled.

  2. No reviews visible

    Sales

    Judge.me is installed, but no reviews or star ratings show. The “Customers Love our products” block appears empty.

    Fix: a review section ready for your real Judge.me reviews.

  3. Your best story isn't on the homepage

    Sales

    Pakistan's first instant coffee brand, founded at 20, with 5% of every order going to Gaza relief. All of that is buried in one blog post.

    Fix: the story and the 5% pledge appear on the homepage and on Our Story.

  4. The hero says nothing specific

    Sales

    “Unmatched versatility, quick, convenient, and made for you” could describe any product.

    Fix: “Café-taste coffee, ready in 30 seconds”, with the brew ratio and a starting price.

  5. No sets or upsells

    Sales

    Matcha buyers aren't offered the chasen, and coffee buyers aren't offered a second jar.

    Fix: “Build your ritual” sets and “Complete your cup” suggestions on every product.

  6. The sold-out tumbler is still promoted

    Sales

    It shows on the homepage with a “Sold out” badge and no way to get notified.

    Fix: sold-out items go last and offer an “Email me when it's back” form.

  7. Contact details are hidden

    Sales

    The phone and email are only on the contact form page, which the menu doesn't link to. A Gmail address also looks less established than an @koffeely.co email.

Speed matters on mobile data

  1. A 1080p video autoplays at the top of the homepage

    Speed

    The hero streams a 7.2 Mbps video, even on phones on mobile data.

    Fix: a still photo. The whole redesign uses 1.2 MB of optimized images in total.

  2. Heavy page code

    Speed

    The homepage HTML is about 330 KB, with 83 script tags and apps loading from 8 or more outside domains (Judge.me alone uses 6).
