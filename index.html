Our story · Since October 2023

It started with an empty shelf

On a grocery run in Karachi, Mahnoor noticed there wasn't a single Pakistani instant coffee brand on the shelves. Store after store, it was the same. So at 20, she started one.

“I wanted Pakistan to have a coffee brand we could trust, one that competes globally but stays true to our values.”
Mahnoor Rajput, founder
5%of every order is donated to relief efforts for Gaza and Palestine.
