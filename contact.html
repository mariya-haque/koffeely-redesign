Delivery and returns

Where do you deliver?

Orders ship from Karachi. Owner to confirm cities covered, delivery time and charges.

Can I pay cash on delivery?

You can pay online at checkout or order on WhatsApp. Owner to confirm COD availability.

Can I return an item?

Because our products are food and drink, we can't take returns on opened items. If your order arrives damaged or incorrect, contact us within 48 hours of delivery and we'll make it right.

How much coffee should I use per cup?

Start with 1 teaspoon (about 2 g) for 200 ml of water or milk. Use 2 teaspoons for iced drinks. See our recipes for more.
